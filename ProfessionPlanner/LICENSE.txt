Trainer learn ranks
-------------------
The skill a trainer asks before teaching a recipe is decided by the server.
For 78 recipes the rank shipped here is the one ForeverWowCraft -
https://foreverwowcraft.com/ - reports as seen at a trainer in WoW Forever
(its route pages, read on 23 November 2026). Those ranks are marked
unconfirmed in the addon. For 121 more Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and
Tailoring recipes the rank is the trainer rank given in the Classic WoW
Forever acquisition text (credited above), where it is the lower one; those
are marked unconfirmed too. A rank the addon reads from a trainer's window in
your own game replaces the shipped one. No endorsement by ForeverWowCraft is
implied.

Vendor stock limits and rank trainers
-------------------------------------
Which recipes vendors sell in limited numbers, and how long they take to
restock, is taken from the CMaNGOS Classic world database -
https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db - which is licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 3. Which trainers teach each profession rank
comes from the same database. Those tables (the `vendorStock` and
`rankTrainers` parts of Data.lua) are offered under the same licence. It describes the original game;
what the addon sees in vendor windows in WoW Forever takes precedence.

Dungeon entrances
-----------------
The positions of dungeon entrances used by the travel arrow (the `dungeons`
part of TravelData.lua) are taken from the same CMaNGOS Classic world
database and are offered under the GNU General Public License, version 3.
They describe the original game world.
